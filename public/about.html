About

A sim racing community built around analog cars (no TC, ESC, or ABS) and technical tracks. Stock cars, vintage GT and formula, Australian Supercars.

Analog Racing Club is an iRacing league for drivers who'd rather race close than qualify well. Three rounds a season, run clean.

Racing conduct

The objective: promote close, clean racing. Leave a car's width when someone is alongside. If you make contact and gain a position, give it back. Contact happens — what matters is how you handle it.

When in doubt, yield. A place lost is cheaper than a race ended.

Eligibility

Open to drivers with a valid iRacing account. License requirements TBD.

Protests

Incident reports go to the league admin within 48 hours of the race, with a reference to the lap and corner.

Join

In iRacing, go to Racing → Leagues → Find a League and search “Analog Racing Club”.
